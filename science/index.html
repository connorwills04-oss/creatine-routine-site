What's in the sticks, and why.

[brand] is two sticks: one for the morning, one for the evening. This page explains what is in each, why it is there, and where the research is settled and where it is not.

Creatine, every day

Creatine is one of the most studied supplement ingredients. It supports muscle when it is taken consistently.

Source: [source needed]

Daily use builds up stores in the body over weeks, not days. That is why this is a routine rather than something you take now and then.

Source: [source needed]

Creatine and the brain

The brain uses creatine in its energy metabolism. Research into the effects of creatine on thinking is active and still developing, so we keep our claims modest: creatine may support brain function as part of a daily routine.

Source: [source needed]

Caffeine and L-theanine, mornings only

The morning stick pairs caffeine with L-theanine, an amino acid found in tea. They are in the morning stick only, so the evening stick stays free of caffeine. Caffeine per stick: [caffeine dose]. L-theanine per stick: [L-theanine dose].

Source: [source needed]

Not for children, or for people who are pregnant or nursing, or sensitive to caffeine. Consider the caffeine you already get from coffee, tea and other sources across the day.

Magnesium, evenings

Magnesium is an essential mineral involved in muscle and nerve function. Research on magnesium and sleep is mixed. We include it in the evening stick because [rationale needed]. Magnesium per stick: [magnesium form and dose].

Source: [source needed]

The evening stick contains no caffeine.

Two sticks instead of one scoop

Splitting the day's creatine across a morning stick and an evening stick is about making the habit easy to keep. Each stick is tied to a moment that already happens, breakfast and the end of the working day, and we are not claiming the split does anything physiological.
