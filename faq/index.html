The routine

What is it?

[brand] is a creatine routine in two sticks: a white stick for the morning and a black stick for the evening. Creatine supports muscle when taken consistently, so the sticks are built around two moments that already happen each day.

When do I take each stick?

Take the white stick with breakfast and the black stick in the evening. The morning stick contains caffeine, so keep it to mornings.

What if I miss one?

Take the next one as normal. Don't double up.

How do I take it?

Tear the stick open into a glass of water and stir.
